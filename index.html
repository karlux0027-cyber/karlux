Every call answered, every message delivered.

KarLux sets up and runs AI phone receptionists for real estate agents and other small professional practices.

What the receptionist does

  • Answers calls you can't take, in your business's name.
  • Takes a message: the caller's name, callback number and reason for calling.
  • Books appointments on your calendar when a caller asks for one.
  • Sends you a summary of each call so you can call back quickly.

KarLux Call Alerts

Clients can choose to receive a text message each time the receptionist takes a call or message for them. Texts go only to clients who have signed up for them, and you can stop them at any time by replying STOP.
